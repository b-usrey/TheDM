⚔️ Classes & Subclasses

Implemented Features & Abilities

Fighter
Masters of martial combat — unmatched action economy and staying power.
Core Features
  • Second Wind

    Bonus action heal for 1d10 + Fighter level once per short rest.

  • Action Surge

    Take one additional action on your turn. Fires automatically when in melee range of an enemy.

  • Extra Attack

    Number of attacks scales with level: 2 attacks at level 5, 3 at level 11, 4 at level 20.

  • Indomitable

    Reroll a failed saving throw. Uses scale with level.

Champion Subclass
  • Improved Critical

    Critical hit range expanded to 19–20.

  • Superior Critical

    Further expanded to 18–20 at level 15.

  • Survivor

    Regain HP equal to 5 + Con modifier at the start of each turn when below half HP.

Fighting Styles Archery (+2 to ranged attack rolls), Two-Weapon Fighting (add ability modifier to off-hand damage).
Ranger
Cunning hunters who blend martial skill with nature magic.
Core Features
  • Favored Foe

    Mark a creature when you hit it. Deal bonus damage (d4/d6/d8 scaling with level) against the marked target. Concentration.

  • Extra Attack

    Two attacks per Attack action from level 5.

  • Roving

    +10 ft. to speed and climbing/swimming speed.

  • Land's Stride

    Moving through difficult terrain costs no extra movement.

  • Nature's Veil

    Bonus action: turn invisible until the start of your next turn. Activates automatically when HP drops below 50%.

  • Foe Slayer

    Add Wisdom modifier to an attack roll or damage roll against your favored enemy once per turn.

Gloom Stalker Subclass
  • Dread Ambusher

    In the first round of combat, gain a bonus attack and +10 ft. speed.

  • Iron Mind

    Proficiency in Wisdom saving throws.

  • Stalker's Flurry

    Once per turn, if you miss an attack, you can immediately make another attack.

  • Shadowy Dodge

    Reaction: impose disadvantage on an incoming attack roll.

Feats Supported Sharpshooter (−5 to attack / +10 to damage tradeoff, ignores cover), Archery fighting style (+2 ranged).
